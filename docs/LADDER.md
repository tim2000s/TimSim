# Which layers change the person, and which only change the run

`FIDELITY.md` reports where the eleven signatures land as each layer is added. That table has
a weakness it does not state: it changes the driving algorithm partway up, running its first
three rungs open loop and its last three under the built-in correction loop. So a reader
cannot tell whether the sensor and site layers move those statistics because of what they do
to the simulated person or because of what a loop does in response.

This is the same ladder walked four times, once per driver, with the layers held constant
within each walk. Two of the four drivers are other people's shipped code called across a
pipe: Boost through a JVM and oref0 SMB through node. Neither knows this package exists, so
agreement between them is not something the package can arrange.

The question is not which driver produces better glucose. It is whether adding a layer moves
a signature the same way regardless of what is dosing. A layer that does is a property of the
person. A layer that does not is an interaction with the controller, and this package should
not describe it as physiology.

Ten adult subjects, 28 days, seed 0, generated by `examples/layer_ladder_by_controller.py`.

## Who survives each rung

| Driver | stock 2008 | +physiology | +behaviour | +sensor | +site |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| basal-bolus | 10/10 | 5/10 | 10/10 | 10/10 | 10/10 |
| trend | 10/10 | 8/10 | 9/10 | 10/10 | 10/10 |
| boost | 9/10 | 8/10 | 10/10 | 9/10 | 10/10 |
| oref | 7/10 | 5/10 | 10/10 | 9/10 | 9/10 |

This constrains what the rest of the document can say, so it comes first. The `+physiology`
rung loses half the cohort under two of the four drivers. Insulin sensitivity that moves
through the day and week to week, without the behaviour layer's rescue carbohydrate, produces
subjects who go low and are never treated. The layers are therefore not independent, and a
cumulative ladder that adds physiology before behaviour is adding a mechanism and its
antidote in the wrong order.

Only 4 of 10 subjects completed all twenty arms. The landing table below is over those four,
so its columns describe one population. Each movement in the agreement table is paired over
the subjects who completed the two rungs it spans, which is more of them and is still like
for like within that comparison.

produce those numbers because it contains nothing capable of producing them.

## Which movements are carried by the person

A movement counts when its paired confidence interval excludes zero. `person` means it does
so under all four drivers and points the same way each time.

Nine of forty-two movements survive. They fall into a pattern.

The behaviour layer carries four, three of them large. Hypoglycaemia recovery shortens by
42.3 minutes under every driver, which is the largest driver-independent effect in the table
and takes the signature from 99.4 minutes into the real range of 50 to 59. Rescue
carbohydrate is what does it, and rescue carbohydrate is a decision the person makes rather
than anything the algorithm can see, which is why no controller changes the answer.

The sensor layer carries three, all large. Compression lows go from nothing to 3.05 per
thirty days and jitter from quantisation to 3.14 mg/dL above it, both inside the real range,
and both under every driver. Nothing an algorithm does can alter how a sensor reports, so this
is the result the design predicts and it is worth having as a check on the method rather than
as news.

The site layer carries two, both small and both autocorrelation, +0.02 at thirty minutes and
+0.06 at sixty. An absorption failure makes the trace more predictable for a few hours, which
is a real effect and a modest one.

The physiology layer carries none. Its largest apparent movement, the diurnal amplitude
falling 18.3 mg/dL, appears under only two of four drivers. That is the finding this document
was written to be capable of producing, and it is a negative one about the package's own work.

## The physiology layer has no signature that can see it

The blank cells in the `drift` row for Boost and oref are not missing data. That statistic is
not computed from the glucose: `run.py` reads it off the built-in controller's learned
correction factor, so it is undefined whenever something else is driving.

Worse, it does not respond to the person. Running the default stack under the built-in loop
with the physiology layer's week-to-week sensitivity term switched off entirely leaves the
signature at 5.77 %CV against 8.69 with it on, a paired difference of +1.26 with a 95%
interval of +/-1.55 over ten subjects. The controller adapts to glucose sitting off target,
which happens whether or not the person's sensitivity moves, and that adaptation is most of
the number. The real range is 8.2 to 21.7, so the in-range verdict in `FIDELITY.md` is largely
the controller reading its own thermostat.

That leaves the physiology layer with one piece of evidence, the diurnal amplitude falling
18.3 mg/dL, which appears under two drivers of four. The eleven signatures are therefore ten
for any purpose involving a person, and the layer whose whole purpose is varying insulin
sensitivity has no statistic in the set that can detect it.

## What that does and does not mean

It does not mean the physiology layer does nothing. It means that at ten subjects, under a
test that demands the effect be visible under all four drivers separately, it cannot be
demonstrated. The rung also has the worst survival of any in the table, so it is measured on
the fewest people. A larger cohort would settle it, and until one is run the package should
not claim insulin sensitivity variation as a demonstrated, driver-independent property.

The one place where a layer clearly makes fidelity worse is hypoglycaemia rebound. The
behaviour layer raises it by 39.7 percentage points under every driver, from 25.5% to 76.7%,
where the real range is 23.2 to 28.4. Stock UVA/Padova is inside that range and the layered
model is nowhere near it. The rescue carbohydrate that fixes recovery time overshoots, and it
does so consistently, which makes it a property of the person as modelled rather than an
accident of one run. It is the clearest single defect this package has in its own terms.

## Reproducing

    python examples/layer_ladder_by_controller.py --subjects 10 --days 28 --workers 8
    python examples/layer_ladder_by_controller.py --table

The two bridged drivers need `bridge/boost/build.sh` and `bridge/oref/build.sh` to have been
run, and each holds a subprocess per subject. The full twenty arms take about ninety minutes
on eight workers.
